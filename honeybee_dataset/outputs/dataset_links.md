# 🌐 Master Directory of Open Datasets & Repositories
### Project: *Apis mellifera* (Honey Bee) × Forage Plants × Geographic Regions

---

## 🐝 1. Adam Tofilski’s Open Datasets & Morphometric Repositories

### A. Southwestern Asia Honey Bee Wing Dataset (2025)
* **Repository (Zenodo):** [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17075125](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17075125)
* **GitHub Analysis Code & Matrices:** [https://github.com/DrawWing/Apis-mellifera-Asia](https://github.com/DrawWing/Apis-mellifera-Asia)
* **Associated Paper (Nature Scientific Data):** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06234-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06234-8)
* **Contents:**
  * **17,457 high-resolution digital wing images**
  * **1,557 colonies sampled**
  * **9 Countries:** Azerbaijan, Cyprus, Georgia, Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Tajikistan, Turkey, Yemen
  * 19 Cartesian landmark coordinate tables (`.csv` / `.txt`), centroid size, cubital index, and R scripts for PCA/CVA discriminant analysis.

---

### B. Pan-European Honey Bee Wing Images & Conservation Tool (2023)
* **Paper & Data Repository (GigaScience / GigaDB):** [https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giad019](https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giad019)
* **GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/DrawWing/DrawWing](https://github.com/DrawWing/DrawWing)
* **Contents:**
  * Standardized, open-access repository of wing photos across European and African subspecies (*A. m. mellifera*, *carnica*, *ligustica*, *intermissa*).
  * Automated landmark detection and subspecies lineage classifier.

---

### C. DrawWing & IdentyWing Platform (Software & Reference Wings)
* **Main Portal:** [http://honeybee.drawwing.org/](http://honeybee.drawwing.org/)
* **Software Download:** [https://drawwing.github.io/](https://drawwing.github.io/)
* **Contents:**
  * Digital reference atlas for honey bee wing venation, anatomical distances, and angle measurements.
  * Free downloadable software for automatic wing measurement, landmark digitization, and cubital index calculation.

## 🌸 2. Plant–Pollinator Interaction & Crop Pollination Datasets

### A. CropPol: Global Database on Crop Pollination
* **Data Repository (Figshare):** [https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.5434550](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.5434550)
* **Associated Paper (Ecology 2022):** [https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.3614](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.3614)
* **Contents:**
  * Dynamic global open database mapping crop visits and pollination efficacy of *Apis mellifera* and wild pollinators.
  * Covers 3,000+ crop fields across 85+ countries and 200+ crop varieties (Apple, Almond, Sunflower, Rapeseed, Coffee, Avocado, Citrus).

---

### B. DoPI (Database of Pollinator Interactions)
* **Live Database:** [https://www.dopi.org.uk/](https://www.dopi.org.uk/)
* **Organizers:** University of Sussex, Eva Crane Trust, British Beekeepers Association
* **Contents:**
  * Over 300,000 recorded interactions between UK/European insect pollinators and host plants.
  * Searchable by plant species (e.g., *Calluna vulgaris*, *Robinia pseudoacacia*, *Trifolium repens*) or bee species.

---

### C. African Honey Bee Forage Plants Database
* **Database & Occurrence Atlas:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01883-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01883-9)
* **Contents:**
  * Geospatial database mapping **1,248 honey bee forage plants** across Africa.
  * Over 1.5 million occurrence records with flowering times, nectar/pollen ratings, and regional vegetation types.

---

## 🍯 3. Floral Resources, Nectar & Pollen Nutritional Datasets

### A. B-GOOD Project Database (Pollen & Nectar Phenology)
* **Repository (Jagiellonian University / EU Horizon):** [https://b-good-project.eu/](https://b-good-project.eu/)
* **Contents:** Open-access spreadsheets detailing nectar volume, sugar concentration, and pollen protein percentage for major European bee forage plants.

### B. USDA Forest Service: Bee Pollen Nutritional Values
* **Archive:** [https://www.fs.usda.gov/rds/archive/](https://www.fs.usda.gov/rds/archive/) (Vaudo et al. Research Data Archive)
* **Contents:** Tabular data (CSV/Excel) on bee host-plant pollen-visitation rates alongside crude protein, lipids, amino acids, and micronutrients.
