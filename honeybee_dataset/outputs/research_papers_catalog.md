## 🐝 Part 1: Key Adam Tofilski Papers by Project Region

### 1. Southwestern Asia (Turkey, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Tajikistan, Cyprus)
* **Title:** *Variation of honey bee wings in southwestern Asia*
* **Authors:** Julita Machlowska, Irfan Kandemir, Ayca Ozkan Koca, Vakhtang Kakhniashvili, Yehya Alattal, Ahmed Alghamdi, Adam Tofilski
* **Year:** 2025 | **Journal:** *Scientific Data* (Nature Portfolio)
* **DOI:** [10.1038/s41597-025-06234-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06234-8) | **Zenodo Dataset:** [10.5281/zenodo.17075125](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17075125)
* **Relevance:** Exact match for Central Asia & Middle East flora regions. Analyzed 17,457 wings from 1,557 colonies across 9 countries. Documents morphometric boundaries between *A. m. anatoliaca*, *caucasica*, *meda*, *syriaca*, *jemenitica*, and *cypria*.

### 2. Central Asia (Kazakhstan)
* **Title:** *In Search of Apis mellifera pomonella in Kazakhstan*
* **Authors:** Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2023 | **Journal:** *Insects / Apidologie*
* **Scholar ID:** `4oJvMfeQlr8C`
* **Relevance:** Investigates the native apple-associated honey bee subspecies (*A. m. pomonella*) in the Tian Shan mountains where wild apple (*Malus sieversii*, ancestor of domestic apple *Malus domestica*) co-evolved with bees.

### 3. Europe (Serbia)
* **Title:** *Influence of honey bee (Apis mellifera) breeding on wing venation in Serbia and neighbouring countries*
* **Authors:** Ljubisa Stanisavljevic, Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2023 | **Journal:** *Journal of Apicultural Research*
* **Scholar ID:** `nU66GSXDKhoC`
* **Relevance:** Direct match for Serbia. Assesses wing venation shifts in native *Apis mellifera carnica* under commercial breeding and migratory beekeeping across Black Locust, Linden, Sunflower, and Rapeseed honey areas.

### 4. Americas (Mexico)
* **Title:** *Reexamination of honey bee Africanization in Mexico and other regions of the New World*
* **Authors:** Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2024 | **Journal:** *Apidologie*
* **Scholar ID:** `VjBpw8Hezy4C`
* **Relevance:** Direct match for Mexico. Uses wing morphometrics to track the hybridization between European lineages and Africanized honey bees (AHB) foraging on Citrus, Avocado, Mesquite, and Coffee.

### 5. Asia (North India / Kashmir Context)
* **Title:** *Morphometric identification of an unknown honey bee colony: an example from north India*
* **Authors:** Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2022 | **Journal:** *Journal of Apicultural Science*
* **Scholar ID:** `nOiSByfp82kC`
* **Relevance:** Validates geometric morphometrics for distinguishing exotic *A. m. ligustica* from native *Apis cerana* in Himalayan valleys (Apple, Almond, Mustard, Saffron).

### 6. North Africa (Algeria / Morocco Context)
* **Title:** *Geographical Variation of Honey Bee (Apis mellifera L.) Populations in South-Eastern Morocco: A Geometric Morphometric Analysis*
* **Authors:** Aglagane Abdessamad, Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2021 | **Journal:** *Insects* (Citations: 22)
* **DOI:** [10.3390/insects12050410](https://doi.org/10.3390/insects12050410)
* **Relevance:** Direct match for North Africa flora (Citrus, Eucalyptus, Thyme, Rosemary, Jujube) and *A. m. intermissa* / *sahariensis*.

### 7. Western & Northern Europe (Lineage M)
* **Title:** *Country specific hybridization of honey bees from lineage M*
* **Authors:** Andrzej Oleksa, Adam Tofilski, et al.
* **Year:** 2024 | **Journal:** *Scientific Reports / Apidologie*
* **Scholar ID:** `uoRD4RTSUPoC`
* **Relevance:** Direct match for your European list: UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Poland, Norway, Belarus, Lithuania, Netherlands. Analyzes introgression into native dark bees (*A. m. mellifera*).

## 🌸 Part 3: Foraging, Food Quality & Environmental Stressors

* **Experimental Wing Damage Affects Foraging Effort and Foraging Distance in Honeybees Apis mellifera**
  * *Author:* Adam Tofilski (2011, *Journal of Insect Behavior*, 31 citations)
  * *Finding:* Empirically links wing wear to foraging distance limits and flower visitation rates.
* **Impact of the quality of food sources on the wing beating of honey bee dancers**
  * *Authors:* Adam Tofilski, et al. (2018, *Apidologie*, 16 citations)
  * *Finding:* Nectar sugar concentration directly alters wing acoustic vibration frequency during waggle communication.
* **Influence of pollen deprivation on the fore wing asymmetry of honeybee workers and drones**
  * *Authors:* Adam Tofilski, et al. (2017, *Apidologie*, 37 citations)
  * *Finding:* Protein and pollen shortages during brood rearing induce fluctuating asymmetry in wing venation.
* **Honey bees are larger and live longer after developing at low temperature**
  * *Authors:* Adam Tofilski, et al. (2015, *Journal of Insect Physiology*, 33 citations)
  * *Finding:* Confirms thermal clines in worker body size between Northern and Southern European ecotypes.
* **Monthly Changes in Honey Bee Forewings Estimated Using Geometric Morphometrics**
  * *Authors:* Adam Tofilski, et al. (2021, *Insects*, 18 citations)
  * *Finding:* Tracks month-to-month morphological shifts in worker cohorts across flowering seasons.
